The Problem

Traditional drawings leave too much room for confusion.

Design decisions are often approved through static drawings, renders, samples, and imagination. Misunderstandings appear later on site, where changes become expensive and trust becomes harder to protect.

TrueScale is designed to close the gap between imagination and execution by allowing people to test the project at real scale, supported by AI intelligence and a controlled decision workflow.

Before TrueScale: drawings, assumptions, revisions, and late-stage uncertainty.
