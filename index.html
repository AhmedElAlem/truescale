The Division

An immersive operating layer between design, execution, and delivery.

Scalixr TrueScale is a specialized division within Scalixr AI focused on immersive spatial decision-making systems for architecture, interiors, development, and future environments.

The platform is designed to reduce uncertainty before construction by allowing people to experience spatial decisions at real scale, supported by AI applications, intelligent agents, sensory environments, execution monitoring, and handover workflows.

Real-scale decision environment A visual layer for spatial testing, walkthroughs, projection, and client confidence.
Core Experience

A clearer journey from concept to real-scale decision.

TrueScale combines immersive walkthroughs, spatial simulation, material selection, kitchen decisions, sensors, cost intelligence, execution verification, and final handover into one connected experience.
